Editorial format: The Post writes individual race editorials in October leading up to election
day, then publishes a composite "Our Choices" summary on election eve / election day. Off-year
elections (1991, 1993, 1995, 1997, 1999, 2003, 2007, 2011, 2013, 2015) have heavy MD/VA state
legislative coverage. Some years (e.g., 1998, 2001, 2002) have very dense composite lists with
100+ candidate records.

The Post has historically endorsed Democrats at the federal/presidential level (Kennedy 1960,
Carter 1980, Mondale 1984, Dukakis 1988, Clinton 1992/96, Gore 2000, Kerry 2004, Obama 2008/12,
Clinton 2016, Biden 2020). State and local race endorsements are more bipartisan, with
significant Republican picks in some races (e.g., Mathias R-MD Senator 1974, Hogan R-MD Gov
2018, Ehrlich R-MD Gov 2006).
