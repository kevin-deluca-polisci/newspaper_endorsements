--- NOTES ---
Coverage: 1968-2010 (continuous biennial general elections, every even year) plus 2022 digital
endorsement summary. Gap: 2012-2020 (no clippings in this batch).

Editorial format: The LVRJ historically published one large composite "election day" or
"election eve" recap listing every endorsement, including the full Clark County local ballot
(county commissioners, judicial races across the 8th Judicial District, justices of the peace,
constables, school board, university regents, plus municipal races for Las Vegas, North Las
Vegas, Henderson, and Boulder City). This produces a high record count per clipping
(40-85 per year typical).

The LVRJ is well-known as a libertarian/conservative-leaning paper. Presidential picks:
- 1968: Nixon
- 1972: Nixon
- 1976: Carter (notable Democratic crossover)
- 1980: Reagan
- 1984: Reagan
- 1988: Bush
- 1992: Bush
- 1996: Dole
- 2000-2008: Bush/Bush/McCain
- (No 2012-2020 in this batch)

Sheldon Adelson purchased the paper in 2015 which strengthened its conservative editorial stance,
but the libertarian streak (opposition to tax increases, bonds, government expansion) was
present throughout the entire dataset's range.
