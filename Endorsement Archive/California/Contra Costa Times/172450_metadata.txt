--- NOTES ---
Contra Costa Times, daily of Walnut Creek/Contra Costa County, California.
Founded 1947 by Dean S. Lesher. Acquired by Knight Ridder 1995, then by
MediaNews Group / Bay Area News Group in 2006. Merged with Oakland Tribune,
Hayward Daily Review, and other East Bay papers in 2016 to form East Bay Times.

Editorially right-of-center for most of this period under Lesher ownership.
Endorsement coverage shows mixed-ticket pattern typical of California papers
(both June primaries and November generals captured for several years).
