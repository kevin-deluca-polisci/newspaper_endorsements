for most cycles). Both June primary clippings and November general election clippings present

Editorial format: FULL OPINION PAGE scans (not isolated endorsement articles), requiring careful

The Times-Advocate's editorial format evolved from compact "T-A recommends" sidebars in the
1960s-70s to fuller multi-paragraph editorials with bolded "Our endorsements" composite sidebars
in the 1980s-90s. The 1990 sheriff endorsement plus full "OUR ENDORSEMENTS" sidebar is
particularly comprehensive.

Three clippings with ZERO endorsements found are legitimate (1972-05-24 was a Chamber of Commerce
position piece, 1974-05-14 was also Chamber, 1981-10-25 + 1984-04-08 + 1988-10-30 were editor
columns about endorsement methodology or letters-to-the-editor pages with no T-A picks).

EDITORIAL IDENTITY (V8 CONFIRMED via deep OCR):
The Times-Advocate was a R-leaning North County daily that reliably endorsed Republicans for
federal office: Nixon (60, 72), Ford (76), Reagan (80, 84), Bush (88 — though "reluctantly"
per editorial headline "Hard to back Bush after slimy campaign"). 1976/1982 SEN picks were
Hayakawa R / Wilson R.

But ETA was MORE pragmatic at state level, endorsing:
- Brown D inc 1978 GOV (cross-party)
- Dymally D 1978 LT GOV
- Cranston D inc 1980 SEN (cross-party)
- Van de Kamp D 1982 AG, Cory D 1982 COMPT, Unruh D 1982 TREAS, March Fong Eu D 1982 SoS
- Feinstein D inc 1994 SEN (cross-party)
- Tony Miller D 1994 SoS

This mixed pattern (reliable R federal + selective D state) reflects North County SD's
pragmatic conservative identity vs LA Times' more liberal pattern.
