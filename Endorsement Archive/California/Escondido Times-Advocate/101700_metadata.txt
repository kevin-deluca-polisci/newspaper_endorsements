EDITORIAL IDENTITY (V8 CONFIRMED via deep OCR):
The Times-Advocate was a R-leaning North County daily that reliably endorsed Republicans for
federal office: Nixon (60, 72), Ford (76), Reagan (80, 84), Bush (88 — though "reluctantly"
per editorial headline "Hard to back Bush after slimy campaign"). 1976/1982 SEN picks were
Hayakawa R / Wilson R.

But ETA was MORE pragmatic at state level, endorsing:
- Brown D inc 1978 GOV (cross-party)
- Dymally D 1978 LT GOV
- Cranston D inc 1980 SEN (cross-party)
- Van de Kamp D 1982 AG, Cory D 1982 COMPT, Unruh D 1982 TREAS, March Fong Eu D 1982 SoS
- Feinstein D inc 1994 SEN (cross-party)
- Tony Miller D 1994 SoS

This mixed pattern (reliable R federal + selective D state) reflects North County SD's
pragmatic conservative identity vs LA Times' more liberal pattern.
