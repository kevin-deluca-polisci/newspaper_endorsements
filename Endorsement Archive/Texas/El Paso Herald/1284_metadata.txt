The El Paso Herald was a separate newspaper from 1881-1931. In 1931, it was sold to Scripps Howard and merged with the El Paso Evening Post to create the El Paso Herald-Post. The Herald and Herald-Post are distinct publications with different editorial identities.

These 1920 clippings were originally filed under the El Paso Herald-Post (145301) in the RA's combined folder, but the Herald-Post did not exist until 1931. The clippings are clearly labeled "El Paso Herald" and have been separated into this distinct paper entry.
