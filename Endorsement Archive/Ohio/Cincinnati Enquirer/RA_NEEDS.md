## 1. MAJOR grid-year correction pass (1958–1974) — the bulk of this folder's errors lived here
Every mid-century year is a sample-ballot clipping (X-mark, ●-dot, or arrow "Enquirer Endorsements"). The original extraction mishandled them systematically. All nine were re-read at high zoom against the ballot image and corrected:

- **At-large years 1958/1960/1962/1964** (Hamilton Co. elected state reps county-wide, "vote for nine"): the extraction had coded the **entire** alphabetical candidate list as Republican/endorsed. In truth each year is ~9 Republicans (marked) + ~9 Democrats (unmarked). Flipped the mis-coded Democrats to D / e=0: **1962 (9 flips), 1964 (5), 1958 (7), 1960 (3 confirmed)**. Recurring name fixes: **Beckman→Reckman, Glasdorf→Glandorf, Grothaus→Groneman, Boggess→Rogoff (Albert A.), Geer→Geier, Shapiro Abraham→Josephine, Luttner→Luttmer.** Added missing D opponents in 1964 (4).
- **1966** (single-member grid): three party errors — **Black (d69) D→R, and the d70 Murdock/Hausman parties were swapped** (the Enquirer cross-party endorsed Democrat **Hausman** over Republican Murdock).
- **1968**: removed 8 bad records (a duplicate Ray, a phantom "Lattimer, Dean", a phantom "Tallman, Walter B.", and a redundant 5-record "JUDGE" block duplicating the Common Pleas judges); **realigned districts** (Schmidt→d66, Mayfield→d67, Murdock→d70, opponents to match); fixed Fields' party (d69 R); **added the omitted William L. Mallory (d72, D, endorsed/unopposed)**. The 3 previously-blank records are resolved: Ray = d65 D not-endorsed (Bechtold won), "Lattimer" = phantom of Luttmer, Conroy = not endorsed (Castle won that Common Pleas seat, already captured).
- **1970**: full **district realignment** (the candidate pairs were right but every district number was shifted — Schmidt→d66, Mayfield→d67, Cruze→d68, Murdock→d70, Scherer→d71, Mallory/Fleming→d72) AND a **direction reversal**: the Enquirer **cross-party endorsed Democrat Mallory over Republican Fleming** (d72) — extraction had it backwards. Added Broxterman (d71 D opponent).
- **1972**: **direction reversal** — **Mallory (D) endorsed over Painter (R)** in the 23rd (extraction had Painter endorsed); also fixed the district 33→23.
- **1974** (arrow ballot): directions were all correct, but districts were off — Anderson→d20, James T. Luken→d24, Mallory→d23, Cibula→d26 (and added two R opponents). Confirmed three cross-party Democratic endorsements (Mallory d23, Luken d24, Rankin d25).

**Net effect:** the at-large years went from "everyone endorsed" to a realistic ~9/9 split; the single-member years now have correctly-numbered districts and verified directions, including five confirmed cross-party Democratic endorsements (Hausman-1966, Mallory-1970/72/74, Luken-1972/74, Rankin-1974) that the extraction had either reversed or buried. Every change is tagged "V9 2026-06-22" in `notes_endorse`.
