Coverage: 1960-1990 (continuous biennial general elections), plus 1996, 2008, and 2010 (two
clippings: the standard pre-election composite AND a digital web "recap" archive that covers
additional Butler County races). Gap: 1992-1994, 1998-2006, 2012+ in this batch.

Editorial format: Composite election-day lists ("Recommended Candidates", "Good Choices for
Voters In State and Local Races", "Clear Choices Offered In Many Local Contests", "Miami Valley
Voters Have Good Choices for Tuesday", "JH Endorsements"-style after merger). 2010 has both a
print election-day composite (20101102) and a digital "A recap of our endorsements" web archive
(20101029) -- some overlap captured by compile dedup.

The DDN was the Democratic-leaning afternoon sister paper to the Republican-leaning Journal
Herald (135102) within Cox Newspapers; both were owned by James M. Cox's family. The two papers
merged in 1986 (the Journal Herald ceased publication and the DDN became the combined paper).

Notable picks:

- Nixon 1972 (notable Republican crossover, matching JH's 1972 endorsement)
- Robert Taft Jr. (R) 1964 Senate over Stephen Young (D) -- another notable R crossover
- Strong support for Gilligan (D Gov 1970/74), Glenn (D Sen 1974+), Metzembaum (D Sen)
- 2010 split slate: Strickland D Gov + Fisher D Sen + most state Ds, but Mandel R Treas, Husted R SoS, OH SC O'Connor + Lanzinger R, plus Boehner R US-8 (heavy Republican area)
