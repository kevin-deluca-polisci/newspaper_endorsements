--- NOTES ---
Coverage: 1960-1984 (continuous even years). Heavy clipping density in 1960 (8 clippings),
1962 (10), 1970 (13), and 1972 (10) reflects the JH's editorial pattern of running ONE editorial
PER RACE throughout October before each election. From 1974 onward, the paper shifted toward
publishing a single composite "JH Recommendations" / "JH Endorsements" list on election day.

Editorial format evolution:
- 1960-1972: one-race-per-day editorials throughout October ("X for [Office]" headline pattern),
  often supplemented by an election-day "For The Record" composite recap
- 1974-1984: single composite election-day "JH Endorsements" list

The Journal Herald was the Republican-leaning sister paper to the Dayton Daily News (the
afternoon Democratic-leaning paper) within Cox Newspapers. The JH supported Nixon 1960/68/72,
Reagan 1980/84, but also crossed over for Gilligan (D) 1970/74 Governor, Glenn (D) 1974/80
Senator, Celebrezze (D) 1982 Governor. The 1986 merger formed the modern Dayton Daily News.
