This folder was flagged for the Cincinnati "at-large false-endorsement" bug (4 slots with 5+ endorsees, no opponents). **All four are legitimate IL cumulative-voting-era statewide slates**, image-verified:
- **1970** (19701028, "Our Choices in Legislative Races"): explicitly endorses "incumbents who should be reelected in the Senate" — 11 Republicans + 7 Democrats by name — plus the House downstate/other-district picks. Every name matches the data.
- **1974** (19741031, "Our list of incumbents deserving of re-election"): 5 Senate + 32 House names — EXACT match.
IL used multi-member House districts (up to 3 reps) until 1980, so multiple same-district endorsees are correct, not the bug.
