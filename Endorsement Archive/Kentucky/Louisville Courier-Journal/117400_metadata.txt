--- NOTES ---
Coverage: 1960-1990 continuous biennial. The Courier-Journal was Bingham-family-owned from
1918 until Gannett's 1986 purchase. Historically considered Kentucky's "Newspaper of Record"
and ranked among the 10 best dailies in the US (per Wikipedia). Won the 1917, 1975, 1991,
and 2021 Pulitzer Prizes (multiple categories).

Editorial pattern: Strongly Democratic-leaning at federal level. Presidential picks captured:
- 1960: Kennedy (D)
- 1964: LBJ (D)
- 1968: Humphrey (D)
- 1972: McGovern (D)
- 1976: Carter (D)
- 1980: Carter (D)
- 1984: Mondale (D)
- 1988: Dukakis (D)

KY-3 Congressional consistently backed Mazzoli (D) throughout the 1970s-90s.

Notable coverage areas: Jefferson County School Board races (heavy 1960s/70s coverage),
Louisville Board of Aldermen, KY state legislative + judicial races. The paper also endorsed
some Indiana races (Senator/House) since Louisville's media market extends into Southern
Indiana — these are coded with state_election=KY for simplicity per the convention of using
the newspaper's state.

The paper's editorial board was particularly active on KY constitutional amendments
(including the 1966 new KY Constitution rewrite and the Broad Form Deed Amendment 2 in 1988
backed YES).
