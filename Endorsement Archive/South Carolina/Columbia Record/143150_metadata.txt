--- NOTES ---
Coverage: 1950, 1952, 1960-1986 even years. The Columbia Record ceased publication April 1, 1988,
so the dataset ends at the 1986 general election.

Editorial format: The Record endorsed each race in a separate editorial throughout the week
before an election, rather than producing a consolidated election-day list. Most clippings
focus on 1-3 races, with detailed reasoning. This produces a high number of clippings per
election year (5-6 in 1972/74/76/80/82/84/86) but a relatively low candidate count per clipping.

The Record was the afternoon (PM) sister paper to The State (Columbia's morning paper) after
State-Record Company was formed in 1945. The two papers had separate editorial boards.

Notable picks:
- 1952: Eisenhower (R) for President
- 1960, 1968, 1972: Nixon (R) for President
- 1976: Ford (R) for President
- 1984: Reagan/Bush (R) for President
- 1986: Campbell (R) for Governor (the Record reflected SC's mid-century shift toward GOP)

The 1960-11-07 clipping was too faded to extract specific endorsements; the 1974-11-01 Governor
race editorial ("An Unhappy Choice") explicitly declined to endorse Dorn or Edwards.
