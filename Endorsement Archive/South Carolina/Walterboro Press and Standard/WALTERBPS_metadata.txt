--- NOTES ---
WEEKLY rural Lowcountry SC paper. Coverage: 1950-2006 with continuous biennial coverage in
most cycles. Each year typically has 1-3 clippings (one per editorial in October leading up to
election day; sometimes a recap on the Wednesday before election).

The Press and Standard had a Democratic editorial stance through the 1980s consistent with the
rural Lowcountry tradition of conservative Southern Democrats: backed Humphrey 1968, Carter
1976, Dorn (D) for Gov 1974, Riley (D) 1978, Mondale/Dukakis at federal level. Gradual shift
toward Republican picks at state/county level from 1990 onward (McBride R Senate 1990,
Whetsell R Sheriff, Flowers, Sanford R Gov 2006 era).

Walterboro is in Colleton County, which was in SC-1 Congressional District before redistricting
moved it to SC-6 (represented by Jim Clyburn D) in the early 2000s.
