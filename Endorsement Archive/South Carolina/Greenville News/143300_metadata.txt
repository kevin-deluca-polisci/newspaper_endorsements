--- NOTES ---
Coverage: 1952-2014 with scattered early decades plus a strong cluster 1990-2014.
The 2018 clipping is the editor's announcement that the paper would no longer endorse
candidates -- captured as a zero-endorsement clipping. Greenville is in SC-4 Congressional
District; Greenville County is the heart of the Upstate region.

The Greenville News was a Republican-leaning paper for most of the modern era and is
considered one of the SC dailies that helped the Upstate develop into a strongly Republican
region in the 1980s onward.

Notable Presidential picks:
- 1952: Eisenhower (R)
- 1984: Reagan (R)
- 1988: Bush (R)
- 2000, 2008: Bush / McCain (R)
- 2012: Romney (R)
- 2016, 2020: not in this batch
- 2018: paper publicly announced it would no longer endorse candidates

Notable other picks:
- Strongly backed Carroll Campbell (R) for SC Governor in 1986 and 1990 re-election
- Backed Thurmond (R) for US Senate consistently
- Knox White (R) for SC-4 in 1988
- One unusual 1954 pick: backed Democrat Edgar Brown over Strom Thurmond's write-in for US Senate
