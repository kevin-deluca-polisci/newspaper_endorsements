--- NOTES ---
Coverage: 1952-2020 with continuous biennial coverage in most decades. The State is the larger
of Columbia's two papers (still in print today; sister Columbia Record ceased 1988). The State
historically endorses each major race in a separate editorial across the week before each
election, plus a composite election-day recap.

Notable Presidential endorsements:
- 1956: Byrd "Independent Democrat" slate (segregationist alternative; rejected Stevenson)
- 1960: Nixon
- 1972: Nixon
- 1980, 1984: Reagan
- 1988: Bush
- 2004: Bush
- 2008: McCain
- 2012: Romney
- 2016: Hillary Clinton -- the paper's first Democratic Presidential endorsement in 40+ years,
        widely noted at the time
- 2020: Biden

Notable other endorsements:
- 1986: Campbell (R) for SC Governor (Campbell won)
- 2010: Sheheen (D) for SC Governor (Haley R won)
- 2014: Sheheen (D) for SC Governor again (Haley R re-elect)
- 1988: Leventis (D) over Spence (R-incumbent) for SC-2 US House ("It's time to change horses
  in Congress")

Editorial format: one race per editorial in October leading up to election day. This produces
many clippings per election year (especially 1970s-1980s; 5-7 clippings per cycle in those
decades).
