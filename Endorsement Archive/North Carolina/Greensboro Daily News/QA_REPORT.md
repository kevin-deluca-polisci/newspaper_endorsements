- 1930: Greensboro News Company acquired the Daily Record
- Long-time publisher: Edwin Bedford Jeffress (1914-1961), then son Charles
- 1965: bought by Norfolk-Portsmouth Newspapers (became Landmark Communications 1967)
- 1980: Sunday consolidation with Record began
- 1983: Saturday consolidation
- March 1984: full merger to News & Record (this archive ends 1982)
