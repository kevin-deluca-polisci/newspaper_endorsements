--- NOTES ---
Coverage: 1960-1984 (continuous biennial general-election Day-Before clippings). No post-1985
clippings because The Charlotte News ceased publication in 1985.

1964 has two clippings (Oct 31 and Nov 2) capturing different pre-election editorials.

Editorial format: long-form ARTICLE editorials ("Tomorrow's Elections", "Summing Up", "The News
Prefers") in the 1960s-70s, transitioning to a more compact LIST-style format in the early 1980s.

The Charlotte News was the afternoon (PM) sister paper to the Charlotte Observer (AM). The two
papers had separate editorial boards and frequently endorsed different candidates -- the News
endorsed Nixon in both 1968 and 1972 while the Observer's endorsement pattern was more mixed.

The folder also contained 12 misfiled Charlotte Observer clippings (1960/62/64/66/70/72/74/76/
82/84/86/88/90 MMDD placeholders matching by SHA-256 to Observer archive files); these were
excluded from this dataset since they belong to newspaper_id 132300.
