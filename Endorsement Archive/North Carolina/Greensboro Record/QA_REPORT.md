## Notes for Downstream Use

- The Greensboro Record was the AFTERNOON paper, founded November 17, 1890 by John Benson, Joseph Reece, and Harper J. Elam
- Owned by Reece 1901-1915; Al Fairbrother and George Crater 1915-1919; Julian Price 1919-1930
- 1930: acquired by Greensboro News Company (parent of Daily News); continued as separate afternoon paper
- More conservative editorial line than morning Daily News
- Editorial line shifted notably in late 1970s: 1976 Ford endorsement, 1978 Helms endorsement, 1980 Reagan endorsement (per agent observation)
- Heavy NC constitutional amendment coverage; many local Guilford County bond issues
- 1984: merged with Daily News into News & Record
