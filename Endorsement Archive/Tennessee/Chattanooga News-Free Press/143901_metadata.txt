--- HISTORICAL NOTES ---
1. **Post-merger Republican-leaning afternoon paper** (1939-1999) — formed from Chattanooga News + Chattanooga Free Press 1939 merger. Carried forward Free Press's R editorial line.
2. **1999 merger**: became Chattanooga Times Free Press after acquiring rival Chattanooga Times.
3. **Editorial line**: Consistent R-leaning — Willkie R 1940, Dewey R 1944+1948, Eisenhower R 1952+1956, Nixon R 1960+1968+1972, Brock R H3 1962+1968+Sen 1970, Baker R H3 1968-74, Howard Baker R Sen 1972, Dunn R Gov 1970, Alexander R Gov 1974.
4. **1944 unusual three-way endorsement** for H 3: paper endorsed BOTH Foster Johnson (Independent) AND Ernest W. Forstner (R) as alternatives to Kefauver D — paper said "thousands of votes cast for Foster Johnson and Forstner in protest against the sinister behind-the-scenes New Deal situation"
5. **1946 Major editorial campaign**: paper led campaign FOR Chattanooga commission-manager form of government referendum against political machine.
6. **1950 Critique of one-party system**: paper editorialized about TN one-party Democrat domination with mostly uncontested races; no specific candidate endorsements in available clipping.
7. **1970 H 3rd State District NO RECOMMENDATION**: paper declined to endorse for state house dist 3.
8. **Two William C/L Carters in 1970**: paper endorsed William C. (Bill) Carter for state 4th + William L. (Bill) Carter for state 6th — different people same nickname.
